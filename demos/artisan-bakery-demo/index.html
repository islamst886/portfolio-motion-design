Micro-Structure & Science

How A 68-Hour Fermentation Shapes The Alveoli.

Standard industrial bread uses fast-acting yeast that creates small, uniform, spongy air pockets that turn stale in 24 hours. Our 68-hour cold fermentation allows wild lactobacilli to synthesize natural aromatic acids while gently opening irregular, gelatinized honeycomb pockets.
