# KINETIC CRAFT STUDIOS — Warm Editorial Light Luxury Portfolio & Agency Hub
**The Master Portfolio Ecosystem for High-Converting Cold Outreach to 100 Physical-Product Leads**

This is your flagship creative studio portfolio website. It is engineered with a soothing, high-converting **warm editorial light luxury palette** (Kinfolk / Architectural Digest style) designed to capture prospect attention, instill calm confidence, and empower clients to immediately commission their digital flagship.

---

## 🎨 Warm Light Editorial Palette
- **Warm Linen Alabaster Base (`#FAF7F2`)**: Welcoming, open, and luminous background eliminating dark gloom.
- **Deep Espresso Charcoal (`#1C1815`)**: High-contrast, sharp typography that reads with effortless clarity.
- **Crisp White Card Surfaces (`#FFFFFF`)**: Clean, elevated card backing with delicate warm sand borders (`#E8DFD3`) and soft pillowy drop shadows.
- **Warm Cognac Terracotta & Roasted Bronze (`#B85D35`, `#8C4724`)**: High-prestige warm accents for primary action buttons and badges.
- **Warm Taupe & Sand Labels (`#6E6459`, `#9C8E80`)**: Gentle secondary metadata text preventing visual clutter.
- **Warm Sunlight Vignette**: Soft amber ambient glow creating a natural daylight editorial atmosphere.
